## Bundled resources

- `resources/models/BlackstarHT1.nam`: trained by this project's trainer on the Blackstar HT-1
  reamp recordings from [Automated-GuitarAmpModelling](https://github.com/Alec-Wright/Automated-GuitarAmpModelling)
  (Wright, Damskägg and Välimäki, Aalto University), which that repository distributes under
  **GPL-3.0**. A model trained on that data is a derived work in the cautious reading; GPL-3.0
  is compatible with distributing the plugin under AGPLv3. If you want different terms,
  replace it with a capture of your own (`training/README.md`).
- `resources/irs/EvilCab4x12.wav`: a synthetic impulse response generated by
  `training/evilamp/make_ir.py`; it is not a recording of any real cabinet.
- `tests/data/parity_*`: generated by `training/evilamp/golden.py` from a random network.

## In practice

- **Personal use**: no restrictions worth worrying about.
- **Sharing the plugin publicly as open source**: publish the source under AGPLv3 (or GPLv3+
  compatible terms for your own code, with the whole combination under AGPLv3).
- **Selling it or closed source**: you need a JUCE commercial licence, and you need to drop
  or replace the HT-1 model.
