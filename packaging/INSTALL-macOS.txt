Evil Amplifier for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel)
==================================================

The builds aren't code-signed, so macOS quarantines them. After copying, clear the
quarantine flag in Terminal (shown for the default install locations):

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Evil Amplifier.app"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Evil Amplifier.vst3"
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Evil Amplifier.component"

Standalone: copy "Evil Amplifier.app" to /Applications and open it. Allow microphone
access when asked (that's how macOS names audio input). Click "Settings..." on the yellow
banner: choose your interface, 48000 Hz, 128 samples, enable only your guitar's input,
and untick "Mute audio input".

Plugins: copy "Evil Amplifier.vst3" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and
"Evil Amplifier.component" (AU, for Logic and GarageBand) to
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/, then rescan in your DAW.
