Evil Amplifier for Windows
==========================

Two ways to use it. You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

1. Standalone app (no DAW needed)
   - Double-click "Evil Amplifier.exe".
   - Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed.
     Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   - A yellow banner says the input is muted (to avoid feedback through speakers).
     Click "Settings..." and set:
       Audio device type:  ASIO (best: your interface's driver, or ASIO4ALL), or
                           Windows Audio (Exclusive Mode), or
                           Windows Audio (Low Latency Mode).
                           Plain "Windows Audio" only offers one sample rate and
                           one buffer size, set by Windows.
       Output / Input:     your audio interface
       Sample rate:        48000 Hz
       Audio buffer size:  128 samples (go up to 256 if you hear crackles)
       Active inputs:      ONLY the input your guitar is plugged into
       Mute audio input:   untick it
   - Use headphones or studio monitors.

2. Plugin in a DAW (Reaper, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Studio One, Bitwig...)
   - Copy the folder "Evil Amplifier.vst3" into
         C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
     (Windows will ask for administrator permission.)
   - Rescan plugins in your DAW, then put "Evil Amplifier" on a mono track with your
     guitar input. Use your interface's ASIO driver in the DAW for the lowest latency.

Using it
   - Plug the guitar into the interface's instrument (Hi-Z / INST) input.
   - Turn up the interface gain until the plugin's INPUT meter peaks around -12 dB when
     you play hard, without lighting the red clip light at the top.
   - Amp > Neural plays the built-in Blackstar HT-1 amp model. "Load .nam..." loads any
     Neural Amp Modeler capture (for example from tone3000.com).
   - Cabinet > "Load IR..." loads a cabinet impulse response (.wav).

Presets you save go to %APPDATA%\EvilAmplifier\Presets.
A log (useful for bug reports) is in %APPDATA%\EvilAmplifier\EvilAmplifier.log.
