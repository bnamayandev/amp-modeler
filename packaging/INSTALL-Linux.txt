Evil Amplifier for Linux (x86-64)
=================================

VST3: copy "Evil Amplifier.vst3" to ~/.vst3/ and rescan in your DAW (Reaper, Bitwig,
Ardour, Carla).

Standalone: run "./Evil Amplifier". With PipeWire, start it as
  PIPEWIRE_QUANTUM=128/48000 pw-jack "./Evil Amplifier"
and pick JACK in Settings for the lowest latency (needs the pipewire-jack package);
ALSA also works. Click "Settings..." on the yellow banner: choose your interface, enable
only your guitar's input, and untick "Mute audio input".

Building from source gives you scripts/install-linux.sh, which also adds an app-menu entry.
